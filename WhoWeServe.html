Who We Serve

Diagenyx works with technical and business teams who need rigorous, independent geoscience to make high-stakes subsurface decisions. Our clients span the full resource development lifecycle — from early exploration through producing asset management and transaction support.

Exploration Teams

Evaluating new plays, prospects, or resource fairways across oil & gas, geothermal, CCUS, and critical minerals. We help define what's there, what it takes to develop it, and what the risk profile looks like.

Asset & Resource Developers

Advancing a known resource toward production or utilization. We provide the technical foundation needed to attract capital, satisfy investors, and move projects forward with confidence.

Operators

Actively drilling, completing, or managing producing assets who need fractured reservoir insight, well planning support, or reservoir quality analysis integrated into ongoing operations.

Technical & Engineering Firms

Leading reservoir engineering, geomechanics, and multidisciplinary consulting shops that need deep geoscience expertise on a project or contract basis. We integrate cleanly into existing technical teams.

Investment & Transaction Teams

Private equity, venture capital, family offices, and lenders requiring independent technical due diligence and A&D support. We speak the language of deals and deliver assessments built for decision-makers, not just technical audiences.

Tribal & Sovereign Energy Entities

Tribes and sovereign nations evaluating or developing subsurface resources on their lands. Diagenyx brings both the technical rigor and the stakeholder awareness these engagements require, including familiarity with federal programs, grant landscapes, and community development goals.

Universities & National Laboratories

Academic and government research partners working on geothermal, CCUS, critical minerals, or subsurface characterization programs. We contribute applied industry expertise and bridge the gap between research and real-world resource development.

Independent Mineral Landowners

Seeking an independent technical perspective on what's beneath their acreage — whether evaluating a lease offer, assessing development activity, or understanding the long-term potential of their mineral interests.
